# Natural Light

Light in Kerbal Space Program 1 as it really falls: from the sky, the ground, the other worlds in the sky,
the flame of an engine and the floodlights of a launch pad; the sun's light in shafts through the air; and
the picture developed as a film camera would develop it. The short version is on the repository's front
page.
