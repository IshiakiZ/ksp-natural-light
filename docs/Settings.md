# Settings

In the Keystone window (the keystone button on the game's toolbar, or Option-K on a Mac, Alt-K elsewhere),
on the Natural Light page. The page also says what the light is at the moment: how high the sun is, how
bright and what colour the sky, the ground and the sunlight are, which other world is shining, what the
engines are lighting and whether the floodlights are on.

| Setting | Installed as | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Natural light | on | Off: the game's own light again, all of it. Every part of the mod goes out, the lamps included. |
| Sky light | 1 | How strongly the sky lights what faces it. Only where there is air, and by day. |
| Ground light | 1 | How strongly the ground, or the planet below, lights what faces it. |
| Light found by rays | on | Rays are cast from the craft in every direction to find what is round it: the sky then lights it only from where it can be seen, and what the rays find lights it from there. Near the ground only. |
| Light thrown back | 1 | How strongly what the rays find lights the craft. |
| Inside the craft too | on | The cockpit, and the crew's portraits, lit by a share of the light outside and by the cabin's own lamps (turned down in the dark), instead of by the game's one flat grey. |
| Darkest | 0.045 | The least light there ever is. The game's own flat light is 0.24 in air and 0.06 in space, by night as by day: raise this towards that if nights are too dark to fly in. |

**The sun**

| Setting | Installed as | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sun through air | if nothing else does | A low sun is dimmer and redder (bluer through Duna's dust). Scatterer does this where it is installed and set to; this mod does it where nothing else does. "always" and "never" overrule that. |
| Shafts of sunlight | on | With the sun in view and something in front of it (a rocket, a tower, a building, the edge of a cloud), its light comes round in shafts through the air. Only where there is air; strongest with the sun low. |
| How strong | 1 | From 0 to 3. |
| Rain, snow, fog and dust in the air | on | With a weather mod installed (Natural Weather), what is in the air veils the distance by as much as the weather says one can see, up to the weather's top, lit as the sky and the ground light it and brighter towards the sun. |
| Low sun | 0.7 | How far the eye keeps pace with a sun that the air has dimmed. At 0 a sinking sun simply dims; at 1 it is turned all the way back up, so that the last light of the day is as strong as noon and only its colour and its long shadows say it is evening. |
| Depth of shadows | 1 | 0: as the game has them, with a quarter of the sunlight coming through. 1: none comes through, and what is in shadow is lit by the sky, the ground and nothing else. |
| Finer shadows up close | on | On a Mac and on Linux: the game's finest shadows drawn in round the craft when the camera is close to it, as the game itself does only on Windows. One part of a craft then throws a clean shadow on another. |

**Other worlds**

| Setting | Installed as | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light of other worlds | 1 | The sunlight thrown back by every world in the sky. 0: none. |
| Night eyes | 1 | How far the eye opens up in the dark. At 0 a full Mun gives its true fifty-thousandth of daylight, which is nothing on a screen; at 1 it is enough to fly by, and cold and silver as moonlight looks. |

**Engines**

| Setting | Installed as | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engine light | on | Running engines light what is round them. |
| How bright | 1 | |

**Film** (done by the TUFX mod; nothing happens where TUFX is not installed, and the page says so)

| Setting | Installed as | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Film look | on | The picture developed as a film camera's would be: off, it is the game's own (and TUFX's profile's) again. In the editors too, at one exposure. |
| Contrast and colour | 1 | How strong the film's contrast is. At 1 the colours are left as the light makes them; above 1 colour is added as well, for a stronger look. 0: the film curve alone. |
| Glow | 1 | The glow round whatever is very bright: a flame, a lamp, sunlight on white paint. 0: the TUFX profile's own, if it has one. |
| The camera's eye | 1 | The exposure follows the view, as an eye or a camera does. In the dark it opens, slowly, and what could not be seen can be (up to a little over three stops, nine times the light). In the light it closes (though not for the sun itself, which burns out as it does in any photograph). And it opens no further than the brightest thing in view can bear: with a floodlit rocket or a sunlit planet in sight, everything dark stays dark, and the stars fade out. 0: one exposure always. |
| Grain in the dark | 1 | A picture pushed to see in the dark is a little grainy. It is faint as installed. 0: none. |
| Creases | 1 | The darkening of creases and of where one thing meets another. 0: none of this mod's. |
| Lens marks | 1 | Slightly darker corners and a trace of colour fringing towards them. |
